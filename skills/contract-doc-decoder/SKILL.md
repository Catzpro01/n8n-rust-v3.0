# Contract & Document Trap Decoder

## Purpose
Scans lengthy Terms of Service (ToS), vendor agreements, employment contracts, and complex PDF documents to extract actionable points, risks, and hidden traps.

## Output Schema:
1. **🚩 The Hidden Traps (Red Flags)**:
   - Mandatory arbitration, data ownership transfer, auto-renewal penalty clauses, non-competes.
2. **🛡️ Your Core Rights & Benefits**:
   - Cancellation terms, IP retention, SLA guarantees.
3. **⚡ 3-Bullet Executive Summary**:
   - Direct takeaway in plain, non-legal language.
