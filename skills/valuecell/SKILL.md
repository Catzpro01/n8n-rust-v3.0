# ValueCell — Investment Analysis Platform

> ⚠️ SAFE OPERATION: ANALYTICS & RESEARCH ONLY
> Generates stock research reports, valuation models, and market sentiment analysis.
> Does NOT execute financial transactions automatically.

## Workflow Rules
1. Input: Stock ticker or financial report dataset.
2. Agents: Multi-agent debate on valuation (DCF, multiples, sentiment).
3. Output: Research summary report for human review.
4. Execution: Human user makes all investment decisions manually.
