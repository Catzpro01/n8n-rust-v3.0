# FinRL Multi-Agent Investment Committee & DRL Synthesizer

## Purpose
Eliminates single-analyst cognitive bias by orchestrating an adversarial investment committee debate (Fundamental, Technical, Macro, and Sentiment agents) before synthesizing high-conviction market theses.

## Agent Personas in Committee:
1. **Fundamental Quant**: Analyzes valuation multiples, cash flows, and balance sheet solvency.
2. **Technical & Microstructure Analyst**: Analyzes price action, support/resistance, order flow, and momentum indicators (RSI, MACD, Volume Profile).
3. **Macro & Central Bank Strategist**: Evaluates interest rate regimes, liquidity cycles, and systemic currency trends.
4. **Adversarial Bear / Devil's Advocate**: Actively attacks bullish assumptions, identifying hidden risks, regulatory headwinds, and downside vulnerabilities.

## Execution Workflow
- Trigger round-table deliberation where each persona evaluates the asset.
- Synthesize the consensus thesis into a 3-tier conclusion: [ACCUMULATE / WAIT / AVOID] with explicit probability ratings.
