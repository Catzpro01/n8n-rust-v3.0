# FinBERT Sentiment & Crowd Psychology Scanner

## Purpose
Quantifies market sentiment, narrative momentum, and crowd euphoria/panic using domain-adapted NLP (FinBERT) and aggregated sentiment indices.

## Core Capabilities
1. **Financial News Sentiment Analysis**:
   - Classifies headlines and market commentary into Bullish, Bearish, or Neutral probability scores using FinBERT/NLP heuristics.
2. **Social Intelligence & Narrative Tracking**:
   - Aggregates discussion velocity on Reddit (r/wallstreetbets, r/cryptocurrency), Twitter/X crypto trends, and community forums.
3. **Sentiment Indices Ingestion**:
   - Ingests Alternative.me Crypto Fear & Greed Index and CNN Stock Market Fear & Greed Index.
4. **Contrarian Sentiment Signals**:
   - Detects extreme greed (market top danger) and extreme fear (capitulation/bottom accumulation opportunity).

## Execution Guidance
- Combine sentiment scores with technical/on-chain data to filter out false FOMO breakouts.
- Highlight sentiment divergence (e.g., price rising on declining social sentiment).
