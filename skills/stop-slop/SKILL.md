# Stop Slop — Writing Polish Filter

## Banned Patterns
- Eliminate "delve", "testament", "beacon", "in the tapestry of", "plethora"
- Strip overly courteous fluff ("I hope this email finds you well")
- Enforce active voice and direct subject-verb phrasing
